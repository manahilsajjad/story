Deep in the Whispering Woods, where the trees leaned close to share secrets, a small lantern lay in the moss. It was glowing softly, though no one had lit it.

Mina found it on a quiet evening. "Hello," she said, because it seemed polite.

The lantern flickered. *"Hello,"* it answered. *"I have been waiting for someone brave."*

## The First Step

Mina was not sure she was brave. But the path ahead glowed gold, and it was easy to follow.
